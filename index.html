Pairs

A memory game. Find every matching pair of pictures.

  • 👆Tap a card to flip it, then tap a second card.
  • ✅If they match, they stay face up. If not, they flip back, so remember where they are.
  • 🌷Clear the board to move up a level. Five levels, each with more cards and more kinds of pictures: flowers, tools, vehicles, animals, food and more.

🎁 Clear all 5 levels and claim 1 GB of mobile data. It is paid for by ad earnings and added to your number within 1 hour of a valid claim.

About Pairs

Pairs is a memory card game. Every card hides a picture, and every picture appears twice. Flip two cards at a time and remember where things are until you have matched them all.
